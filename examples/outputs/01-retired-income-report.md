**Capacity for loss.** Band 4 of 5. This is a different question from your attitude: it is what your finances can absorb, not what you would be comfortable with. You told us you could absorb a permanent loss of up to 15%, that your reserve outside this investment covers 6 - 12 months, and that your regular commitments take less than 20% of your income.

**Which of these decided the outcome.** We do not average these assessments against one another: a willingness to take risk cannot substitute for the ability to bear a loss. We take the most restrictive. Here that was **the objective you set for this money** (band 2), which is why the recommendation sits at band 2.
