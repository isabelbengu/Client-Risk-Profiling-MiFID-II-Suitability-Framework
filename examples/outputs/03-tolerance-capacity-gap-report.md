## 2. How this meets your objectives and personal circumstances

**Investment term.** You told us you intend to hold this investment for 7 to 10 years. No specific commitment. No withdrawals.

**Knowledge and experience.** We assessed this at band 3 of 5. It is based on the instruments you have actually held, how often you have transacted, your background, and the worked examples you answered - not on how you rated yourself. This determines which types of product we may recommend to you (complexity tier 2 and below), because you must be able to understand the risks involved.

**Attitude to risk.** Band 5 of 5. You said the largest twelve-month fall you would accept without changing your plan is up to 35%, and that in a 20% market fall you would invest more while prices are lower.

**Capacity for loss.** Band 3 of 5. This is a different question from your attitude: it is what your finances can absorb, not what you would be comfortable with. You told us you could absorb a permanent loss of up to 15%, that your reserve outside this investment covers less than 3 months, and that your regular commitments take 40% - 60% of your income.

**Which of these decided the outcome.** We do not average these assessments against one another: a willingness to take risk cannot substitute for the ability to bear a loss. We take the most restrictive. Here that was **your capacity for loss** (band 3), which is why the recommendation sits at band 3.

- **Concentration in a single mandate.** This investment is a large share of your financial assets. Concentrating that much in one strategy adds risk that is separate from the strategy itself, so we have limited the level of risk.

- **Tolerance materially exceeds capacity.** What you are willing to risk and what your finances can absorb are two different things, and here they are some way apart. We have to work to the lower of the two.
  <small>Control CC03 (refer); MiFID II Art. 25(2); ESMA GL4 para 51. Recorded detail: risk tolerance band 5 exceeds capacity band 3 by 2.</small>
- **Stated loss tolerance exceeds absorbable loss.** The loss you told us you would accept is larger than the loss you told us you could absorb without changing your plans. We have used the amount you could absorb.
  <small>Control CC04 (cap); MiFID II Art. 25(2) - ability to bear losses. Recorded detail: stated loss tolerance 35% exceeds absorbable loss 15%.</small>
- **Return expectation not achievable at the accepted risk level.** The return you are expecting is higher than the level of risk you have accepted can reasonably deliver. We would like to go through the relationship between the two with you again.
  <small>Control CC07 (refer); ESMA GL2 para 23; GL4 para 51. Recorded detail: client expects a band 5 return from a band 3 portfolio.</small>
