## 2. How this meets your objectives and personal circumstances

**Investment term.** You told us you intend to hold this investment for more than 10 years. No specific commitment. No withdrawals.

**Knowledge and experience.** We assessed this at band 1 of 5. It is based on the instruments you have actually held, how often you have transacted, your background, and the worked examples you answered - not on how you rated yourself. This determines which types of product we may recommend to you (complexity tier 0 and below), because you must be able to understand the risks involved.

**Attitude to risk.** Band 5 of 5. You said the largest twelve-month fall you would accept without changing your plan is up to 35%, and that in a 20% market fall you would invest more while prices are lower.

**Capacity for loss.** Band 3 of 5. This is a different question from your attitude: it is what your finances can absorb, not what you would be comfortable with. You told us you could absorb a permanent loss of up to 30%, that your reserve outside this investment covers 3 - 6 months, and that your regular commitments take 40% - 60% of your income.

**Which of these decided the outcome.** We do not average these assessments against one another: a willingness to take risk cannot substitute for the ability to bear a loss. We take the most restrictive. Here that was **your capacity for loss** (band 3), which is why the recommendation sits at band 1.

- Profile band 3 was reduced to 1 in product terms: knowledge gate tier 0 and a 15-year horizon rule out the higher band.
- Equivalent products at the same risk band and sustainability classification were compared on ongoing cost and complexity; the least costly was selected (Art. 54(9) DR 2017/565, ESMA GL9 paras 91-95).
