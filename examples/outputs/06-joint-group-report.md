It is based on the instruments you have actually held, how often you have transacted, your background, and the worked examples you answered - not on how you rated yourself. This determines which types of product we may recommend to you (complexity tier 1 and below), because you must be able to understand the risks involved.

**Attitude to risk.** Band 2 of 5. You said the largest twelve-month fall you would accept without changing your plan is up to 35%, and that in a 20% market fall you would invest more while prices are lower.

**Capacity for loss.** Band 5 of 5. This is a different question from your attitude: it is what your finances can absorb, not what you would be comfortable with. You told us you could absorb a permanent loss of up to 50%, that your reserve outside this investment covers more than 12 months, and that your regular commitments take less than 20% of your income.

**Which of these decided the outcome.** We do not average these assessments against one another: a willingness to take risk cannot substitute for the ability to bear a loss. We take the most restrictive. Here that was **the objective you set for this money** (band 2), which is why the recommendation sits at band 2.
