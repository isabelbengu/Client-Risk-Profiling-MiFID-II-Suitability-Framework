## 2. How this meets your objectives and personal circumstances

It is based on the instruments you have actually held, how often you have transacted, your background, and the worked examples you answered - not on how you rated yourself. knowledge band 5 capped at 4: highest instrument tier actually held is 2. This determines which types of product we may recommend to you (complexity tier 3 and below), because you must be able to understand the risks involved.

**Attitude to risk.** Band 4 of 5. You said the largest twelve-month fall you would accept without changing your plan is up to 20%, and that in a 20% market fall you would do nothing, but review whether my plan still holds.

**Capacity for loss.** Band 5 of 5. This is a different question from your attitude: it is what your finances can absorb, not what you would be comfortable with. You told us you could absorb a permanent loss of up to 30%, that your reserve outside this investment covers 6 - 12 months, and that your regular commitments take 20% - 40% of your income.

**Which of these decided the outcome.** We do not average these assessments against one another: a willingness to take risk cannot substitute for the ability to bear a loss. We take the most restrictive. Here that was **the objective you set for this money** (band 3), which is why the recommendation sits at band 3.
